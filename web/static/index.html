        Your car's radar

        The radar measures from the front of the car, the comma from the windshield. Two values measured on your car turn a radar reading into a distance from the comma. Leave the first one empty when the car has no radar.

        • From the radar to the comma: park in front of a wall and measure, along the car, the wall to the comma and the wall to the radar; the difference is this.
        • Correction: added to every radar reading, for example −3 when the radar sits 3 cm behind the bumper. The tests at known distances will tell the real value.
